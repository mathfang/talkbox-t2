Why
No new window to manage, and no metaphor to learn. The agents are ambient and permanent — you zoom, you see them, they were never hidden.
Tradeoff
The most expensive to build — it fights macOS Spaces for the whole desktop. Zoomed out, no single agent is legible enough to actually work with.
