The Table

peers, not processes

Zoom out and you are not above a grid of windows — you are above a table. Each agent has a sheet in front of it and a presence beside it. Work moves by sliding a sheet across, not by switching a pane.

Why
The only direction where agents read as company rather than compute. Sliding a sheet across is a handoff you can feel — coffee-shop geometry, at four seats.
Tradeoff
The heaviest metaphor. A table is not a filesystem, so real app windows have to be flattened into "sheets" — and it could read as twee if the craft slips.
